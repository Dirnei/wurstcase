# Faktenbuch research

Candidate facts for the Faktenbuch and the Act 4 counter, researched 2026-09-27. Every source
below was opened and supports the fact as worded, but **check each one yourself before it ships**
(design rule 3.7). Confidence: **high** = primary or official source says it plainly; **medium** =
secondary source, contested number, or interpretation debated.

Suggested unlock trigger in brackets.

---

## 1. Production chains

### Soy

**S1. Only 7% of soy is eaten directly by people** · high · [first soybean field]
77% of the world's soy goes to animal feed; just 7% becomes tofu, soy milk, edamame or tempeh
(data 2018).
DE: 77 % der weltweiten Sojaernte landen im Futtertrog, nur 7 % essen Menschen direkt.
Source: Our World in Data, "Soy" – https://ourworldindata.org/soy

**S2. German soy area almost tripled** · high
15,800 ha (2016) → 43,300 ha (2025), +174%; over half (52.7%) in Bavaria. Still only 0.3% of
German farmland. Germany still imports ~3.4 Mt of soybeans a year, ~26× its own harvest.
DE: Die Sojafläche in Deutschland stieg 2016–2025 um 174 %, mehr als die Hälfte liegt in Bayern.
Source: Destatis, PM July 2026 –
https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/07/PD26_N052_51_41.html

### Tofu

**T1. Tofu is about 2,000 years old** · medium · [first tofu press]
First recorded in China's Han dynasty (202 BC – AD 220). Legend credits Prince Liu An, but the
evidence is thin – word it as legend.
DE: Tofu ist seit der Han-Dynastie belegt; der Legende nach erfand ihn Prinz Liu An.
Source: Wikipedia, "Tofu" – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofu

**T2. Benjamin Franklin wrote about "tau-fu" in 1770** · high
In a letter to John Bartram (11 Jan 1770) he sent soybeans and described a Chinese "cheese" made
of them.
DE: 1770 beschrieb Benjamin Franklin Tofu in einem Brief als chinesischen Käse aus Sojabohnen.
Source: Smithsonian Magazine –
https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/ben-franklin-may-be-responsible-bringing-tofu-america-180968495/
(primary letter: founders.archives.gov/documents/Franklin/01-17-02-0010)

### Seitan

**SE1. Seitan: 1,500-year-old food, 1961 name** · high · [first seitan kitchen]
Wheat gluten appears in the Chinese farming encyclopedia Qimin Yaoshu (AD 535) and became a meat
substitute for Buddhist monks (Chinese miànjīn, "wheat tendon"). The word "seitan" was coined in
1961 by macrobiotics promoter George Ohsawa.
DE: Weizengluten ist seit 535 n. Chr. belegt; das Wort „Seitan“ erfand George Ohsawa erst 1961.
Source: Wikipedia, "Wheat gluten (food)" – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat_gluten_(food)
Myth: "seitan = fresh protein" is a guess, not established.

### Leberkäse → Leverkas

**L1. Bavarian Leberkäse needs no liver** · high · [Leverkas unlocked]
Under the German Leitsätze, "Leberkäse" sold outside Bavaria must contain liver – unless it is
called "Bayerischer Leberkäse", which may be liver-free and need not even come from Bavaria.
Stuttgarter Leberkäse must contain at least 5% liver (Wikipedia, medium).
DE: „Bayerischer Leberkäse“ darf ganz ohne Leber auskommen – und muss nicht mal aus Bayern stammen.
Sources: Lebensmittelklarheit –
https://www.lebensmittelklarheit.de/informationen/erlaubt-bayerischer-leberkaese-ohne-leber ;
Wikipedia (de) "Leberkäse" – https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leberkäse
Myth: "Leberkäse never contains liver" – only true for the Bavarian kind.

**L2. Neither liver nor cheese** · medium
"Leber" probably derives from "Laib" (loaf), and Bavarian "Kas" means any paste-like mass.
Fun bonus: in Dutch it is called **"leverkaas"** – almost the game's "Leverkas".
DE: „Leber“ kommt wohl von „Laib“, „Kas“ heißt im Bairischen „breiige Masse“.
Sources: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leberkäse ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leberkäse

### Currywurst (Tofu-Wurst / "Currywurst ohne Wurst")

**C1. Herta Heuwer, Berlin, 1949** · medium · [first Tofu-Wurst sold]
Heuwer said she invented the sauce on 4 Sep 1949 at her stand in Berlin-Charlottenburg, from
ketchup and curry powder obtained from British soldiers. Her "Chillup" was a registered trademark
(1959), not a patent. Write "nach eigenen Angaben" – the date is disputed.
DE: Herta Heuwer erfand nach eigenen Angaben am 4. September 1949 die Currywurst-Soße.
Source: Wikipedia (de) "Currywurst" – https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currywurst

**C2. The VW currywurst is a car part** · high
Since 1973 Volkswagen makes its own currywurst, sold as "Originalteil" 199 398 500 A (ketchup:
…500 B); 8.33 million in 2023. A wheat-based vegan version exists since 2010.
DE: Die VW-Currywurst ist offiziell ein Originalteil mit der Teilenummer 199 398 500 A.
Source: Wikipedia, "Volkswagen currywurst" – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_currywurst
Unverified: "VW sells more sausages than cars" – leave out.

### Oats

**O1. Oatly started at Lund University** · high · [first oat mill]
Oatly grew out of research by food scientist Rickard Öste at Lund University, Sweden; founded 1994.
DE: Oatly entstand aus Forschung an der Universität Lund und wurde 1994 gegründet.
Source: Wikipedia, "Oatly" – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oatly

**O2. Oat drink vs. cow's milk** · high · [first Hafer-Cappuccino]
Per litre, cow's milk needs ~12× the land (8.95 vs 0.76 m²), ~13× the water (628 vs 48 L) and
~3.5× the greenhouse gases (3.15 vs 0.90 kg CO₂e). Note: soy drink uses even less water (28 L).
DE: Pro Liter braucht Kuhmilch etwa 12-mal so viel Fläche und 13-mal so viel Wasser wie Haferdrink.
Source: Our World in Data (data: Poore & Nemecek 2018, Science) –
https://ourworldindata.org/environmental-impact-milks

**O3. Samuel Johnson's oat insult** · high
His 1755 dictionary: oats – "a grain, which in England is generally given to horses, but in
Scotland supports the people."
DE: Hafer – „ein Getreide, das in England an Pferde verfüttert wird, in Schottland aber die
Menschen ernährt“ (Samuel Johnson, 1755).
Source: Mental Floss –
https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/538473/funny-definitions-from-dictionary-of-the-english-language-samuel-johnson
Myth: the famous "fine horses / fine people" comeback has no reliable source.

### Germany

**G1. Meat consumption rising again** · high
54.9 kg per person in 2025 (+1.4 kg, third rise in a row), still below 63.8 kg in 2011. Poultry at
an all-time high of 14.7 kg.
DE: 2025 aß jede Person im Schnitt 54,9 kg Fleisch – weniger als 2011 (63,8 kg).
Source: BLE, PM 7 Apr 2026 –
https://www.ble.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/260407_Fleischbilanz.html

**G2. Meat substitute production doubled** · high
124,900 t in 2025, more than double 2019 (60,400 t), but –1.2% vs. 2024. Meat production is still
worth ~70× as much.
DE: 2025 wurden 124.900 t Fleischersatz produziert, mehr als doppelt so viel wie 2019.
Source: Destatis, PM May 2026 –
https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/05/PD26_N033_42.html

**G3. 2% vegan, 37% flexitarian** · high
BMLEH Ernährungsreport 2025: 2% vegan, 7% vegetarian, 37% flexitarian. Daily meat eaters fell
from 34% (2015) to 24% (2025). Most-bought alternatives: plant milk 85%, tofu 81%.
DE: 2 % ernähren sich vegan, 7 % vegetarisch, 37 % flexitarisch.
Source: https://www.bmleh.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Broschueren/ernaehrungsreport-2025.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3
Myth: some sites say "4% vegan" for this report – the report says 2%.

## 2. Lebenshof animals

### Chickens [first chicken rescued]

**H1. Chickens blush** · high
Cheeks and earlobes redden with excitement or fear and pale when calm – "blushing is not
exclusive to humans."
DE: Hühner erröten: Bei Aufregung werden Wangen und Ohrläppchen röter, in Ruhe blasser.
Source: PLOS ONE 2024 – https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0306601

**H2. Chickens see colours we can't** · high
Four colour cone types (violet/near-UV, blue, green, red) vs. our three.
DE: Hühner haben vier Farbzapfen-Typen statt unserer drei.
Source: Kram et al. 2010, PLoS ONE – https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2813877/

**H3. At least 24 different calls** · high
DE: Hühner haben mindestens 24 verschiedene Laute.
Source: Marino 2017, Animal Cognition, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10071-016-1064-4 (summary:
https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2017/01/170103091955.htm)

**H4. Hens wait for the jackpot** · high
Hens chose a longer wait when it meant a much bigger food reward (self-control).
DE: Für eine viel größere Futterportion warten Hennen lieber etwas länger.
Source: Abeyesinghe et al. 2005, Animal Behaviour – https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anbehav.2004.10.011

**H5. Mother hens feel their chicks' stress** · high
When chicks got a harmless puff of air, the hen's heart rate rose and she clucked more – one
building block of empathy.
DE: Wird den Küken harmlos Luft zugepustet, steigt der Puls der Glucke.
Source: Edgar et al. 2011, Proc. R. Soc. B – https://www.bristol.ac.uk/news/2011/7525.html

**H6. Chicks place numbers left to right** · medium
3-day-old chicks link small quantities with left, large with right. Interpretation disputed – do
not say "chicks can count".
Source: Rugani et al. 2015, Science – https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa1379

**H7. 23 years vs. 37 days** · high
Oldest chicken: Muffy, 23 years. German broilers are slaughtered at ~37 days on average (1950s:
~120 days to reach 1.5 kg).
DE: Hühner können über 20 Jahre alt werden – ein Masthuhn lebt rund 37 Tage.
Sources: https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/739669-oldest-chicken-ever ;
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9583157/ ;
https://www.landwirtschaft.de/einkauf/lebensmittel/tierische-lebensmittel/gefluegelfleisch

### Pigs [first pig rescued]

**P1. Pigs played a video game** · high
Pigs learned to steer a cursor with a joystick using their snout, well above chance.
DE: Schweine lernten, mit der Schnauze per Joystick einen Cursor zu steuern.
Source: Croney & Boysen 2021, Frontiers in Psychology –
https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.631755/full
Myth: "pigs beat dogs/chimps at it" – the paper says no such thing.

**P2. A translator for grunts** · high
An algorithm told positive from negative situations for 92% of 7,414 calls from 411 pigs. Happy
grunts are short and drop in pitch.
DE: Ein Algorithmus erkennt an 92 % der Grunzer, ob es einem Schwein gut oder schlecht geht.
Source: Briefer et al. 2022, Sci. Rep. – https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/945428

**P3. More smell genes than dogs** · high
~1,113 working smell-receptor genes vs. dog ~872 and human ~388.
DE: Schweine haben rund 1.113 Riech-Gene – fast dreimal so viele wie Menschen.
Source: Nguyen et al. 2012, BMC Genomics – https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3499278/

**P4. Mud is their air conditioning** · high
Pigs lack working sweat glands; wallowing cools them and also works as sunscreen and against
parasites. Not "dirty" at all.
DE: Schweine können kaum schwitzen – Schlamm ist Klimaanlage und Sonnenschutz.
Source: Bracke 2011 – https://phys.org/news/2011-05-wallowing-mud-temperature.html

**P5. Sows call their piglets to dinner** · medium
The sow grunts faster and faster; then milk flows for only 10–15 seconds.
DE: Die Sau ruft ihre Ferkel mit immer schnellerem Grunzen – die Milch fließt nur 10–15 Sekunden.
Source: The Pig Site – https://www.thepigsite.com/articles/lactation-in-motion

**P6. 23 years vs. 6 months** · high
Oldest pig: Baby Jane, 23 years. German fattening pigs are slaughtered at 110–120 kg, aged 5–6
months.
DE: Schweine können über 20 Jahre alt werden – Mastschweine werden mit 5–6 Monaten geschlachtet.
Sources: https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2021/8/23-year-old-pig-baby-jane-becomes-oldest-pig-in-captivity-ever-669916 ;
https://www.landwirtschaft.de/tier-und-pflanze/tier/schweine/wann-ist-ein-schwein-schlachtreif

### Cows [first cow rescued]

**K1. Veronika, the cow who uses a brush** · high
An Austrian Brown Swiss cow scratches herself with a deck brush – bristles for her back, smooth
handle for sensitive spots. First documented tool use by a cow.
DE: Kuh Veronika aus Österreich kratzt sich mit einem Besen – die erste Werkzeug-Kuh.
Source: Osuna-Mascaró & Auersperg 2026, Current Biology –
https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/03/260326075611.htm

**K2. Potty-trained calves ("MooLoo")** · high
11 of 16 calves learned to pee in a latrine – as well as children. Done in Germany (FBN
Dummerstorf).
DE: Forschende brachten 11 von 16 Kälbern bei, eine „Kuh-Toilette“ zu benutzen.
Source: Dirksen et al. 2021, Current Biology – https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/927878

**K3. Eureka moments** · medium
Young cattle that solved a task got visibly excited – higher heart rate, jumping, bucking.
DE: Rinder, die eine Aufgabe knacken, machen Freudensprünge.
Source: Hagen & Broom 2004, via Marino & Allen 2017 –
https://www.animalbehaviorandcognition.org/uploads/journals/17/AB&C_2017_Vol4(4)_Marino_Allen.pdf

**K4. Every cow has her own voice** · high
DE: Jede Kuh hat eine eigene Stimme – ob sie sich freut oder gestresst ist.
Source: Green et al. 2019, Sci. Rep. –
https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2019/12/19/stand-out-from-herd-how-cows-communicate.html

**K5. 330° vision** · high
Nearly all-round view with a blind spot directly behind. Bulls don't react to red – they react to
movement.
DE: Kühe haben einen Rundumblick von mindestens 330°.
Source: Marino & Allen 2017 (PDF above)

**K6. Calves learn better with a buddy** · high
Pair-housed calves adapted to changed rules; calves housed alone kept failing.
DE: Kälber, die zu zweit aufwachsen, lernen flexibler.
Source: Gaillard et al. 2014, PLOS ONE – https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0090205

**K7. Favourite partners calm them** · medium
Separated with their preferred partner, heifers had lower heart rates than with another familiar
cow. PhD thesis, not peer-reviewed; say "Lieblingspartnerin", not "best friend for life".
Source: McLennan 2013 –
https://nectar.northampton.ac.uk/id/eprint/6466/1/McLennan_Krista_2013_Social_bonds_in_dairy_cattle_the_effect_of_dynamic_group_systems_on_welfare_and_productivity.pdf

**K8. 20 years vs. 3–4 years of milking** · high
Natural life expectancy ~20 years; productive life in high-yield dairy countries ~3–4 years.
DE: Kühe könnten rund 20 Jahre alt werden – Milchkühe werden meist nach wenigen Jahren geschlachtet.
Source: animal (Cambridge) 2020 –
https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal/article/review-overview-of-factors-affecting-productive-lifespan-of-dairy-cows/EF3D233CB84CE8AE36769A1966C67C34

### Scale facts

**D1. Livestock 60%, wild mammals 4%** · high
By mass, livestock are ~60% of all mammals on Earth, humans 36%, wild mammals ~4%. Farmed
poultry outweighs all wild birds about 3 to 1.
DE: Nutztiere machen rund 60 % der Säugetier-Biomasse aus, Wildtiere nur etwa 4 %.
Source: Bar-On, Phillips & Milo 2018, PNAS – https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6016768/

**D2. 83% of farmland, 18% of calories** · high
Meat, aquaculture, eggs and dairy use ~83% of farmland but provide 37% of protein and 18% of
calories.
DE: Tierische Produkte belegen 83 % der Agrarfläche, liefern aber nur 18 % der Kalorien.
Source: Poore & Nemecek 2018, Science – https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaq0216 ; OWID:
https://ourworldindata.org/global-land-for-agriculture

**D3. Livestock emissions: 12–16.5%** · high (that the sources say it; the number is contested)
- FAO 2023: 6.2 Gt CO₂e/yr (2015), ~12% of human emissions –
  https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/new-fao-report-maps-pathways-towards-lower-livestock-emissions/en
- FAO 2013: 7.1 Gt, 14.5% (the classic number) –
  https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/Major-cuts-of-greenhouse-gas-emissions-from-livestock-within-reach/en
- Twine 2021: 16.5% should be the minimum – https://doi.org/10.3390/su13116276
- Animal-based foods cause 57% of food-production emissions, twice plant-based (Xu et al. 2021,
  Nature Food) – https://doi.org/10.1038/s43016-021-00358-x

Show a range or pick one source per statement; don't mix system boundaries.

**D4. ~70% of antibiotics go to farm animals** · medium
73% of antimicrobials sold worldwide (93,300 t in 2017). OWID says "around 70%", with a lower
estimate of ~66%.
DE: Rund 70 % aller Antibiotika werden bei Nutztieren eingesetzt.
Sources: Tiseo et al. 2020 – https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7766021/ ;
https://ourworldindata.org/antibiotics-livestock

**D5. Chick culling banned in Germany since 2022** · high
Before 1 Jan 2022 about 40 million male chicks were killed each year after hatching. Good as a
"stopped" counter – proof that demand and law can change things.
DE: Seit 1. Januar 2022 ist das Kükentöten verboten; zuvor rund 40 Mio. Küken pro Jahr.
Source: BMLEH – https://www.bmleh.de/DE/themen/tiere/tierschutz/tierwohl-forschung-in-ovo.html

## 4. Myths – do not use

| Claim | What the sources actually support |
|---|---|
| "Pigs are as smart as 3-year-olds" | No study gives a human age; Marino & Colvin 2015 say "similar to dogs and young children". |
| "Pigs beat dogs/chimps at video games" | Pigs learned a joystick task; the paper calls it not optimal for pigs. |
| "Pigs recognise themselves in mirrors" | They used a mirror to find food (2009); a 2014 replication largely failed. |
| "Chickens are as smart as 7-year-olds" | Refers to one narrow skill (transitive inference). |
| "Chicks can count" | They map quantities left-to-right; interpretation disputed. |
| "Cows have best friends for life" | PhD thesis; preferred partners calm them, bonds fade after regrouping. |
| "Big Bertha the cow lived to 49" | Unverified (AnAge). |
| "Bulls hate red" | They react to movement. |
| "Leberkäse never contains liver" | Only "Bayerischer Leberkäse" may be liver-free. |
| "Heuwer patented Chillup" | Registered trademark, 1959. |
| "VW sells more sausages than cars" | Not verified on a primary page. |
| "4% of Germans are vegan" (Ernährungsreport 2025) | The report says 2%. |
| "Tofu eaters drive rainforest soy" | 77% of soy goes to animal feed, 7% to direct human food. |
